Dust, pollen, bird droppings, and industrial soot block sunlight from hitting the solar cells. This is called soiling loss. In dry climates, soiling can reduce power generation by 1.5% per week, cumulating to over 25% if left uncleaned.

Our AMC includes: Scheduled panel washing (twice a month), semi-annual electrical tests (string voltage, insulation resistance), annual thermal imaging of panels/connections, priority troubleshooting, and inverter service sweeps.

We use infrared thermal cameras to scan panels. Defective solar cells or loose cable connectors generate heat and appear as bright "hot spots" under thermal imaging. Catching these early prevents cell degradation and fire hazards.

Typically, twice a month is ideal to prevent soiling losses. In dusty industrial zones or near highways, weekly cleanings may be required. We offer automated robotic dry-cleaning solutions as well.
