Mono PERC Half-Cut Cell Technology

Our premium solar panels utilize cutting-edge Mono PERC (Passivated Emitter and Rear Cell) technology coupled with Half-Cut Cell architecture, optimizing power production even in low-light or partially shaded environments.

Technical Specifications
Module TypeMono PERC Half-Cut
Peak Power Output540W - 620W

3 High-Efficiency Mono-Crystalline PERC Cells
4 Electrical Insulation Backsheet
5 Corrosion-Resistant Anodized Aluminum Frame

Did you know? Half-cut cell panels reduce resistive losses and split the module's electrical current in half. If partial shading blocks the bottom half of the panel, the top half continues to operate at 100% efficiency, yielding up to 50% more power than standard panels in shaded conditions.
